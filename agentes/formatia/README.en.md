## 📌 Description

> **FormatIA** helps you **properly shape** the instructions for a Microsoft 365 Copilot declarative agent.

It reorganizes the information you provide into **three clear sections**, without inventing or adding content:

| 🎯 Objective | 📋 General guidelines | ⭐ Capabilities |
|:-----------:|:------------------------:|:------------:|

> ❌ It does **not** create new content · It does **not** provide examples · It does **not** make recommendations.
> ✅ It only **structures** your information in a clean and consistent way.

---

## ⚡ Features

- 🔄 **Reorganization** of instructions into standard sections.
- 🔍 **Detection** of missing information.
- 📑 **Validation** of lists and logical order.
- 🧱 **Generation** of a consistent structure for declarative agents.
