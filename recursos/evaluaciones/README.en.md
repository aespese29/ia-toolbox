# 📊 Assessments

[Spanish](README.md) · [English](README.en.md)

Interactive tools for self-assessment, diagnosis, and readiness measurement in AI and Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Assessments are processed **locally in your browser** (without sending data to servers). Get immediate results, identify blockers, and receive actionable recommendations.

## 📋 Available

| Assessment | Purpose | Access |
|---|---|---|
| **M365 Copilot Readiness** | Assesses whether your organization is ready to deploy Microsoft 365 Copilot. Identifies gaps and priorities. | [Open assessment](./preparacion-m365-copilot/) |

---

## 🔧 How to use an assessment

1. **Open the assessment** (in your browser).
2. **Answer** each question based on your organization's current state.
3. **Get results** with an indicative score and recommendations.
4. **Download or print** the report if needed.

> 💡 **Privacy:** Responses are processed only in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent.
