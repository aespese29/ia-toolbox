## 🎤 Are you a speaker who wants to share your session?

Create a folder named after your session (lowercase with hyphens) and add:
1. `README.md` → summary, content, speaker, and contact details
2. `presentacion.pdf` → session file
3. `cover.jpg` or `cover.png` → cover image

Then send us the link at **aespes@adrianespes.com**.
