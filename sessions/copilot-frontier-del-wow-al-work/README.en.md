# Copilot Frontier: from "wow" to "work" in your organization

[Spanish](README.md) · [English](README.en.md)

![Copilot Frontier: from "wow" to "work" in your organization](cover.jpg)

Session delivered at **CollabDays Madrid 2026** (June 4, 2026, Microsoft Iberia HQ) about the Microsoft 365 Copilot Frontier program: what it is, how to enable it, and above all how to turn preview features into real work inside the organization.

## Content

- What is Frontier? Frontier enterprises and early access to AI capabilities
- The lifecycle of a capability: from private preview to general availability
- Frontier graduates: which capabilities are already GA
- From "wow" to "work": three real-world scenarios chaining Frontier tools
- Copilot Cowork, Work IQ, skills, and plugins
- Which tasks does it really accelerate? Research, synthesis, and data analysis
- Backstage: prerequisites, minimum enablement, policies, and rollout model
